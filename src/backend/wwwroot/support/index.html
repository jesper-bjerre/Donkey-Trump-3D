Donkey Trump 3D Support

Support and operation: Jesper Bjerre.

Contact

Contact Jesper through the game's GitHub Issues. GitHub requires an account to post. Issues are public: do not post personal details, private report contents or installation credentials. For a private matter, ask for private contact without including those details.

Playing the game

Climb the girders, dodge barrels and reach Motzfeldt. Move and climb with the touch controls; use Jump to clear hazards. Play in landscape. Installed gameplay works without an internet connection.

Sound and Haptics can be changed from the menu. With Sound enabled and audible volume, game audio plays even when the iPhone's silent switch is on.

Highscores

The online list holds the top 100 results. Low-scoring cartoon starters keep at least ten entries available. Publishing a result is optional: enter a name and tap Submit. Names and scores on the list are public. There is no player login and no queued upload after a failed connection. Your personal best stays on your iPhone.

Report a name

Tap the flag beside an entry, choose a reason and tap Send Report. My Reports lets you explicitly check a receipt's status. Jesper Bjerre checks reports every working day and is responsible for responding by the same local time on the next Monday–Friday working day in Europe/Copenhagen.

An offensive entry can be removed and its app installation blocked from publishing further results, including under a different name. Local play and reporting remain available. Older results may lack an attributable installation and can only be removed. Reinstalling can reset an installation block; scores are not verified game replays.

If a response is lost, the report or score may already have been saved. Check Status for a report or refresh the list for a score. A missing or expired receipt does not prove that no earlier report was saved. If the reporting service is unavailable, use the contact link above.

Privacy and removal

Read the privacy policy for stored information, retention and removal. You can report an entry containing personal information directly in the app.
