## description

Climb. Dodge. Rescue Motzfeldt.

Take control of Jumpman Løkke in a satirical 3D arcade game. Climb ladders, jump over rolling barrels and reach Motzfeldt at the top while Donkey Trump tries to stop you. Every rescue brings another level and faster barrels.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Use landscape touch controls: drag on the left to move and climb, and tap on the right to jump. Game controllers and keyboards are supported too.

CHASE YOUR BEST
Earn points for jumping barrels and completing rescues. Keep your personal best on your iPhone, or choose to publish a name and score to the online global top 100. Publishing is optional, and no player account is required.

READY FOR A QUICK GAME
Installed gameplay works offline. Adjust sound and haptics from the menu. Online highscores require an internet connection; failed submissions are not queued for later upload.

Donkey Trump 3D is an original satirical parody. It is not affiliated with any game publisher or politician.

## notes

No account or sign-in is required. The game is played in landscape. Tap Start Game, then use the left half of the screen to move and climb ladders and the right half to jump. The intro can be skipped. Installed gameplay works offline.

Online highscores are optional. Players explicitly submit a chosen name and score. The highscore list provides a flag action for reporting an entry and My Reports for checking a report receipt. Support and privacy links are available in the app.

This game is a satirical parody and is not affiliated with any game publisher or politician.
